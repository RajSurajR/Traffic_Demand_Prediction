5-fold LightGBM ensemble with cyclical time encoding.

This version builds a stronger regression pipeline using KFold validation and more expressive temporal features. It standardizes timestamps, creates cyclical time variables, adds geohash-region features, and averages predictions across five fold-specific models.

Main steps:
- fix train/test timestamp mismatch
- impute Temperature with median and categorical gaps with 'Unknown'
- extract hour, minute, and time_in_minutes
- add time_sin and time_cos for daily periodicity
- create geohash_zone_4 and geohash_zone_5 plus historical demand averages
- train LGBMRegressor in 5-fold cross-validation with early stopping
- ensemble fold predictions into the final submission

Validation outcome: average cross-validation R² ≈ 0.96229.
